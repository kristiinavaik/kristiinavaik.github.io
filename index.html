About

I am a researcher at the University of Tartu, working in digital humanities and computational linguistics. I am part of the Center for Digital Text Scholarship (DigiTS).

I completed my PhD at the University of Tartu in 2024. My dissertation, Beyond Genres: A Dimensional Text Model for Text Classification, was supervised by Kadri Muischnek and Kairit Sirts.

My full academic CV is available in the Estonian Research Information System (ETIS).

Research

My research has addressed:

  • Text classification and dimensional approaches to text variation.
  • Estonian language processing and linguistic annotation.
  • Multilingual language resources and word representations.
  • Computational approaches to textual materials in the humanities.
